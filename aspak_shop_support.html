Frequently asked questions

How do I contact support?

Email us at devwithdelta@gmail.com or call +91 98338 78819. Please include your registered phone number and order details when contacting us.

How do I update my delivery address?

You can add or update your address inside the app. Location access is optional and is used only to help show your current location on the map while adding your address.

Can I use the app without location permission?

Yes. You can deny location permission and manually enter your delivery address.

Do you support online payment?

No. Poiser Fresh Chicken currently supports cash on delivery only. We do not collect debit card, credit card, or online payment details in the app.

How can I delete my account?

You can request account deletion directly inside the app from the account/settings section. After verification, your information is deleted from our active database within 30 days, unless we are legally required to retain specific records.

Why do I receive notifications?

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send order updates and app notifications. You can turn off push notifications from your device settings.

What app services are used?

The app uses Firebase Phone Authentication for OTP login, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications, Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports, Firebase Analytics for app usage analytics, and Google Maps for address selection.

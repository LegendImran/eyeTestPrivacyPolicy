Frequently asked questions

How do I report a bug?

Send us an email at imran.shisheo@gmail.com with a description of the issue and, if possible, screenshots of the problem and the device you're using.

How do I request a new feature?

We welcome suggestions. Please send your ideas to our support email and tell us what you'd like to see — it genuinely helps shape the app.

Why does the app show ads?

Drishti is free to use and is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. If you ever see an ad that seems broken or inappropriate, email us the details so we can look into it.

How do in-app purchases and subscriptions work?

Any purchases are processed and billed securely by your device's app store (Google Play or the App Store). To view, manage, or cancel a subscription, open your app store account's subscription settings.

How is my data handled?

We only collect what's needed to run and improve the app. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I contact support?

Email imran.shisheo@gmail.com. Including your device model and app version helps us help you faster.

Your privacy matters. For details on how we collect, use, and protect your information, please read our Privacy Policy.
